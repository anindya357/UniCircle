identity | CUET identity, location and address
Reviewed: 2026-10-08
Review after: 2027-04-08
Sources:
- https://cuet.ac.bd/about/cuet
- https://www.timeshighereducation.com/world-university-rankings/chittagong-university-engineering-technology

Facts:
CUET means Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology, a public
engineering university in Bangladesh. It is not the University of Chittagong
(CU), BUET, KUET, DUET, or India's Common University Entrance Test.
Its postal address is Pahartoli, Raozan, Chattogram 4349, Bangladesh.
Chittagong and Chattogram refer to the same city; Raojan/Raozan and
Pahartali/Pahartoli are spelling variants. The campus covers about 171 acres.
The university's general registrar email is registrar@cuet.ac.bd.

history | CUET history and university status
Reviewed: 2026-10-08
Review after: 2027-04-08
Sources:
- https://www.timeshighereducation.com/world-university-rankings/chittagong-university-engineering-technology
- https://cuet.ac.bd/about/history

Facts:
CUET began as an engineering college in 1968. In 1986 it became Bangladesh
Institute of Technology, Chittagong (BIT Chittagong). It became Chittagong
University of Engineering and Technology in 2003. Undergraduate teaching is
in English. Its academic areas include engineering, technology, architecture,
planning and the supporting sciences.

Facts:

five academic faculties and four research institutes. It describes 12
undergraduate degree-awarding departments. These are different counts: all
academic departments include supporting-science and postgraduate departments.
Some older summaries say 17 departments; that should not replace the reviewed

faculty members/teachers has not been established in this knowledge base.
The UniCircle directory's 12 listed departments are an app subset, not CUET's
complete academic structure. Planned expansion is not counted as existing.

institutes | CUET internal research institutes and their names
Reviewed: 2026-10-08
Review after: 2027-01-08

- https://itn.buet.ac.bd/web/notice/cuet-announces-new-diploma-program-in-wash-management-under-gsgs/

Facts:
CUET has four named research institutes: Institute of Energy Technology (IET);
Institute of Earthquake Engineering Research (IEER); Institute of Information
and Communication Technology (IICT); and Institute of River, Harbor and
Environmental Science (IRHES). IRHES is an institute, not a student club.
BUET's International Training Network independently announced CUET IRHES's
July 2026 postgraduate diploma in WASH Management. IT Business Incubator and
Research and Extension may appear on the same web navigation page, but are
not additional academic institutes in this four-institute count.

incubator | Where is CUET IT Business Incubator and what does it do?
Reviewed: 2026-10-08
Review after: 2027-01-08
Sources:
- https://ossbhtpa.gov.bd/park-info
- https://itbi-cuet.com/
- https://mapcarta.com/N11389783321

Facts:
IT Business Incubator (ITBI), CUET is on the CUET campus in Pahartoli, Raozan,
Chattogram. It is associated with Bangladesh Hi-Tech Park Authority and supports
technology entrepreneurship, startup incubation, training and industry links.
Older documents and maps call it Sheikh Kamal IT Business Incubator.
The reviewed OpenStreetMap-derived office marker is approximately latitude
22.46082, longitude 91.97268, close to the CUET Post Office and PME Building.
This is an approximate map marker, not a surveyed entrance or a guaranteed
walking route. CUET ITBI is not the separate Chandgaon/C&B Mor training center
or the Agrabad software technology park in Chattogram city.

Facts:
